Search Pg

This project is a user-friendly and responsive website aimed at simplifying the process of finding PG (Paying Guest) accommodations. The platform allows users to search for PGs by selecting their city, area, and gender preferences, ensuring personalized results. On the same page, users can view a list of PGs that match their criteria, along with hostel-related images to provide a better understanding of the accommodations. One of the key features of this project is the ability for users to book beds directly through the platform, making the process convenient and efficient. Additionally, Google Maps integration helps users locate the PGs easily and understand their proximity to essential places. The website also includes important features like contact information, a help center, and other essential tools to assist users with their needs. For property owners, the platform offers dedicated options to list and manage their PG accommodations, creating a comprehensive and inclusive system for both users and owners.
